# Domains NetHop never writes to the hosts file: a profile that maps one of them, or any of its subdomains, is
# rejected. They are what Windows and its apps need to stay updated and secure, sign-in and certificate services,
# and the biggest online services, whose redirection Microsoft Defender reports as a threat (HostsFileHijack).
#
# One domain per line, lowercase; "#" starts a comment. A domain covers its subdomains too, but not other domains:
# "google.com" covers "mail.google.com", not "googleadservices.com". Changes go through a pull request.
